CareCart: A Solution for College Students & Grocery Shopping Struggles

V Bansal, P Singh, R Li, C Chandu, A Ramachandran, A Zhang

Paper Human-Computer Interaction, UC Davis · Sep 2025 · 5 pages

2025

For many college students, grocery shopping is stressful: a limited weekly budget, the pressures of university life, and health needs ranging from medical conditions to food intolerances. CareCart is a student-centered platform that suggests affordable, diet-friendly alternatives and offers a community space for recipe sharing and peer support. Using HCI methods and design thinking, we interviewed students, identified pain points, and prototyped from sketches to a high-fidelity platform, with usability heuristics guiding a design built for clarity and ease of use.

Methods

User interviews Thematic analysis Affinity diagramming Personas Journey mapping Prototyping Usability testing

USC ACT Lab

Pathfinding for Snow Blowing

V Bansal · ACT Lab, USC Viterbi (PI: Nora Ayanian)

Poster USC Viterbi SHINE 2021

2021

Route planning for an autonomous snow-blowing robot built on a JetBot (NVIDIA Jetson Nano). The final system localized with QR codes and planned with a finite-state algorithm. It followed a zig-zag pattern that clears a driveway without scouting it first, which saves battery and time. The project also explored collision avoidance with PyTorch object detection, visual odometry, and camera-based snow-depth estimation.

Methods

Path planning Jetson Nano Visual odometry OpenCV PyTorch

Multi-Robot Path-Finding

V Bansal · ACT Lab, USC Viterbi (PI: Nora Ayanian)

Poster USC Viterbi SHINE 2020

2020

Multi-agent path finding: moving many robots to their own goals in a shared space without collisions, like robots moving goods around a warehouse. Agents were trained with deep reinforcement learning in PyTorch, with convolutional layers feeding an Advantage Actor-Critic model. The work progressed from single-agent path finding to many agents in environments with obstacles.
